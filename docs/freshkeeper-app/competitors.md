## 1. Summary — the shape of the field

The household pantry tracking space is moderately populated with legacy utility apps, but heavily fragmented by usability friction. The category leader, [NoWaste](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1112404094), established standard pantry inventory management but relies heavily on manual item entry, creating high user churn. Emerging AI recipe apps like [PicMeal: AI Meal Planner](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6470000000) focus on dietary macros rather than zero-waste expiration alerts.

**Price floor observed**: Free basic tier at [NoWaste](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1112404094) / Pro subscription at $2.99/month or $19.99/year.
