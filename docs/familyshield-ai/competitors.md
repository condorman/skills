## 1. Summary — the shape of the field

The scam-defense space is a **sandwich with no room in the middle**. On the bottom, free, credible, brand-backed tools — [Norton Genie](https://www.malwarebytes.com/solutions/best-scam-detection-tools), [Bitdefender Scamio](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/consumer/scamio), indie [ZeroScam](https://zeroscam.io/) — cover "is this message a scam" at zero price, which caps the category's price floor at €0 for the exact mechanic a new entrant would lead with. On the top, telco-scale incumbents ([Robokiller](https://robokiller.com/) at a reported $2.0M/mo, [Truecaller](https://mashable.com/roundup/best-robocall-blocking-apps)) own call-blocking with proprietary number-reputation databases that cannot be rebuilt by a solo developer. On the flank, [Carefull](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/getcarefull.com) locked up senior financial monitoring through bank distribution ([Edward Jones partnership](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/edward-jones-introduces-carefull-financial-safety-platform-to-help-families-protect-what-matters-most-302808126.html)). The recurring category complaint is billing/cancellation practices on paid security subscriptions, not missing features — no wedge there. **There is no opening at solopreneur scale.**

**Price floor observed**: free at [Bitdefender Scamio](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/consumer/scamio), [Norton Genie](https://www.malwarebytes.com/solutions/best-scam-detection-tools) and [ZeroScam](https://zeroscam.io/) — any pricing model has to argue against €0 with a security-brand trust deficit.

## 4. Causes of death & reasons for success

### 🪦 Why the dead ones died
No dead competitors were verified in this session's searches — the notable pattern is the opposite: the space's products survive as **free loss-leaders funded by adjacent businesses** (antivirus suites, telco licensing, bank distribution). That is itself the structural finding, and it is recorded in `ideas_log.md`: *consumer scam-defense monetizes only as a loss-leader or via institutional distribution; the standalone direct-to-consumer middle does not exist.* No `## Category Red Flags` line was added, since this is a discovery-mode observation from one session, not a deep-dive finding.

### 🏆 Why the winners win
