---

#### 1. Core Problem & Latent Friction
- **Discovered Friction**: Real-world stained glass crafting is a highly coveted cozy aesthetic craft, but requires $300–$600+ in specialized equipment (diamond glass cutter, grinder, soldering iron, toxic lead solder, flux, copper tape), dangerous glass handling with shard hazards, and dedicated workshop ventilation. Meanwhile, existing "stained glass" games on Steam and Mobile (*Glass Masquerade*, *Stories in Glass*) are static jigsaw puzzle assembly games rather than physical craft simulators.
- **Target Audience**: Cozy game enthusiasts (*Unpacking*, *Sticky Business*, *Townscaper* players), ASMR/satisfying video fans, DIY art lovers, and remote workers seeking relaxing ambient desk focus companions.

---

#### 2. The "Why Now?" Factor (Tech Enabler)
- **Recent Tech Breakthrough**: WebGL/Mobile 2D & 3D real-time screen-space caustics shaders, hardware-accelerated fluid/extrusion mesh rendering (for soldering line flow), and lightweight generative AI prompt-to-vector sketch pipelines (allowing players to input prompt ideas that convert into score-line patterns).
- **Why It Couldn't Be Built Earlier**: Previous mobile devices lacked the GPU performance to render real-time light refraction, caustics, and volumetric light beams in screen-space alongside interactive physics without heavy battery drain.
