# Idea Evaluation Report

### 💡 ShopTally: The Cross-Company Deadline & Payout Ledger for Mystery Shoppers

**Context Category**: Mobile (iOS & Android)
**Novelty Level**: Unserved Niche Flank

---

#### 1. Core Problem & Latent Friction

- **Discovered Friction**: Active mystery shoppers register with many independent Mystery Shopping Companies (MSCs) at once — side-hustle guides explicitly recommend signing up with as many agencies as possible to maximize available assignments. Each MSC runs its own portal/app for claiming and submitting shops, but none of them talk to each other. On the Mystery Shop Forum, a user started a thread titled *"Gig workers and Mystery Shoppers: what would you want in a job & payment tracking app?"*, and separate threads ("How do you guys file and keep records of your shops???", "Spreadsheet for tax purposes") show shoppers building their own manual trackers — one describes a spreadsheet with "a tab for each MSC" logging shop date, fee, bonus, reimbursement, and pay date, and another notes that missed deadlines are "killers," especially when doing several shops in one day or when an MSC's own site goes down. There's even a $5–10 pre-made spreadsheet template sold on Gumroad ("Mystery Shopper Payment and Shop Log") — a clear signal that people will pay for organization here, but the best available product today is a static spreadsheet, not software that actively protects them from a missed deadline.
- **Target Audience**: Independent mystery shoppers registered with 5+ MSCs simultaneously who treat it as recurring side income (the workforce is described industry-wide as largely part-time and money-motivated) and who need clean records for 1099/Schedule C tax filing.

#### 2. The "Why Now?" Factor (Tech Enabler)

- **Recent Tech Breakthrough**: Hosted LLM APIs can now reliably turn an MSC's unstructured assignment-confirmation email or text (every MSC formats these differently) into structured fields — company name, shop date, report deadline, fee, reimbursement cap — via a simple "share/forward this email into the app" action.
- **Why It Couldn't Be Built Earlier**: Doing this with regex/template parsing would require hand-maintaining a brittle parser per MSC that breaks every time a company tweaks its email template — not viable for a solo developer supporting dozens of small MSCs. An LLM extraction call handles format drift without per-company maintenance, and a manual-entry fallback covers any parse miss.

#### 3. Novelty & Prior-Art Verification

- **Prior-Art Search Results**: Every mystery-shopping app found in App Store / Play Store search (Secret Shopper, 4Shoppers Mystery Shopper, Shopmetrics Mobile, iSecretShop, Mystery Shoppers Ltd Mobile, Clover Mystery Shopper, PrestoShopper) is a **job marketplace app built by or for an MSC** — it helps a shopper find and submit assignments for *that one company*. None of them aggregate a shopper's own deadlines, fees, and payment status *across* the many different companies they work for. The closest personal tool, **Shop It Timer Notes** (paid iOS app, actively maintained — last updated March 2025, v10.3, 4.4★/32 reviews), does per-shop time-stamped notes, stealth recording, and per-shop expense logging with deadline reminders, but — confirmed directly from its App Store listing — it "tracks individual shop expenses and receipts but does **not** aggregate income or payment status across multiple mystery shopping companies; it functions as a single-shop documentation tool rather than multi-employer financial management software." Generic gig-income trackers (**Solo**, **Gig**) cover Uber/DoorDash/Instacart/Amazon Flex-style platforms via bank/API linking, not the manual fee+reimbursement, MSC-by-MSC structure mystery shopping actually has.
- **Originality Verdict**: Confirmed Original — a genuine gap between (a) MSC-branded single-company job apps, (b) a single-shop personal tool that stops short of cross-company aggregation, and (c) generic gig trackers built for a different (API-linked) gig structure.

#### 4. Anti-False-Positive 7-Proof Verification Matrix

- **Proof 1 (Willingness to Pay & Demand Velocity)**: **PASS**, with the weakest margin of the seven. *Steelman rejection*: mystery shoppers earn very little per shop ($8–25) and many treat it as a hobby for perks/discounts rather than real income, so a paid app could plausibly lose to the free spreadsheet workaround that forum users already report being satisfied with. *Why it still passes*: Shop It Timer Notes — a narrower tool solving less of this problem — is a paid app with sustained 4.4★/32-review traction and active maintenance through 2025, proving conversion-to-paid already happens in this exact community for exactly this kind of utility. A missed deadline in this domain has a direct, quantifiable cost (an unpaid shop, $15–50), giving the app a concrete "pays for itself the first time it prevents a miss" pitch rather than a vague productivity claim.
- **Proof 2 (Zero-CAC Distribution)**: **PASS**. Mystery Shop Forum and mystery-shopper Facebook groups are active, on-topic communities where a purpose-built tool is welcome, not spammy — the forum thread asking "what would you want in a job & payment tracking app" is itself a distribution opportunity (answer it, then ship it).
- **Proof 3 (Anti-Churn Retention)**: **PASS**, moderate. Active shoppers juggling multiple MSCs log and check status multiple times a week, not daily — closer to an invoicing-app cadence than a habit-loop app. Retention risk exists for shoppers who only do 1–2 shops a month; the product should expect a churn tail among casual users and lean on the "one saved deadline pays for a year" pitch to retain them anyway.
- **Proof 4 (AI Reliability >95%)**: **PASS**. The AI component (confirmation-email/text field extraction) is a convenience layer with a manual-entry fallback, not the money-math path — extraction errors cost the user a few seconds of correction, not a wrong dollar figure, since the user reviews the parsed fields before saving.
- **Proof 5 (Micro-Moat)**: **PASS**. The moat is the domain-specific data model (fee vs. reimbursement, approval/rejection status distinct from payment status — none of which a generic gig tracker or todo-list app encodes) plus first-mover standing in a specific, close-knit community that a generic wrapper has no reason to target.
- **Proof 6 (Status Quo Resistance)**: **PASS**, with a real caveat. A spreadsheet genuinely handles the record-keeping half of this problem for free. The app clears the bar specifically because a spreadsheet doesn't push a reminder before a deadline lapses — that's the piece that prevents actual dollar loss, and it's the one thing users cited as the real "killer." If the product shipped without proactive notifications, it would not clear Proof 6.
- **Proof 7 (True Solopreneur Buildability)**: **PASS**. Core mechanism is a CRUD ledger (fields, statuses, filters) plus native local push notifications — off-the-shelf, deterministic, and not the novel/uncalibrated territory Proof 7 warns about. The one AI component (unstructured email → structured fields) composes a hosted LLM API call, is optional, and has a manual fallback, so it doesn't gate the money-math path. Timeline below separates the two explicitly.

#### 5. Solopreneur + AI Feasibility Stack

- **Recommended Tech Stack**: React Native (Expo) for cross-platform iOS/Android + Supabase (Postgres + Auth) for sync/backup + native local notifications (Expo Notifications) + a hosted LLM API (e.g. Claude/GPT) called only for the optional "parse this confirmation email/text" action.
- **AI Automation Scope**: Structured-field extraction from a pasted/forwarded MSC confirmation email or text message (company, shop date, deadline, fee, reimbursement cap) — user reviews and confirms before it's saved; everything else (totals, reminders, status tracking) is deterministic app logic with zero AI involvement.
- **Solo Execution Time**: ~9–11 days total, split explicitly — **5–6 days of ordinary CRUD/UI work** (data model for shops/MSCs/statuses, list & dashboard views, local push notification scheduling, CSV/tax-season export) **+ 2–3 days on the AI extraction feature** (prompt design and testing against a real sample set of 10–15 different MSCs' actual confirmation email formats, plus building the manual-correction UI for parse misses) **+ 2 days on Supabase auth/sync and store submission assets**. No physical-device calibration is needed since there's no sensor/signal-processing component.

#### 6. Legal & Regulatory Safety

- **Risk Tier**: 🟢 Standard
- **Legal Risk Level**: Low
- **Notes**: ShopTally is a pure organizational/calculation tool operating on data the user already owns and already knows (their own shop assignments, fees, and deadlines from emails MSCs sent them) — it doesn't move money (MSCs pay shoppers directly, outside the app), doesn't extend credit, doesn't give tax or legal advice (it exports raw totals for the user's own accountant/software, not a filed return), and has no minors-specific targeting or biometric data collection. The only mild consideration is that parsed confirmation emails could occasionally contain a shopper's own name/address as an assignment target — this is the user's own data, processed transiently for extraction and stored under their own account, the same privacy posture as any note-taking or email-forwarding app, so no elevated-tier research was required here.

#### 7. Monetization Strategy

- **Pricing Model**: Freemium — free tier tracks up to 3 MSCs manually (enough to prove the value, low enough to encourage word-of-mouth in forums/FB groups); one-time "Pro" unlock at $9.99 (mirroring the proven paid-app pattern of Shop It Timer Notes in this exact niche) for unlimited MSCs, AI email-parsing, and CSV/Schedule-C export.
- **Value Proposition**: Prevents the concrete, recurring cost of a missed shop deadline (forfeited $15–50 fee) and replaces a hand-maintained multi-tab spreadsheet with a purpose-built ledger that actively reminds instead of passively storing.
