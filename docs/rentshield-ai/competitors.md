## 1. Summary — the shape of the field

The tenant inspection landscape is fragmented into two distinct segments:
1. **B2B Property Management Solutions**: B2B platforms like [RentCheck](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rentcheck/id1134017691) and [zTenant](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ztenant/id1491039980) serve landlords and property managers who want tenants to complete remote inspections for the landlord's records. They require property operator accounts or landlord invitation codes.
2. **Niche Consumer Utility Apps**: Standalone renter-focused utilities like [Tenant Inspect](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenant-inspect-rental-report/id6450515110) ($19.99 upfront paid app) and [Tenant Inspection Log](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenant-inspection-log/id6782419245) (free app, local storage) exist but suffer from poor UI/UX, lack of cryptographic verification, or lack of AI automated defect assistance.

The primary competitive force remains **DIY / Status Quo inertia** (unorganized camera roll photos with no timestamping or structured PDF export).

**Price floor observed**: Free tier at [RentCheck](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rentcheck/id1134017691) (for invited tenants) / $19.99 upfront at [Tenant Inspect](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenant-inspect-rental-report/id6450515110).

## 4. Causes of death & reasons for success

### 🪦 Why the dead ones died / quiet ones stay niche
- **Paid upfront model ($19.99)**: Consumers refuse to pay $19.99 upfront for an app without testing the room walkthrough flow first.
- **Landlord dependency**: Apps built exclusively for B2B property managers fail to empower independent tenants moving into private rentals or dealing with non-tech-savvy landlords.

### 🏆 Why the winners win
- **[RentCheck](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rentcheck/id1134017691)** wins on B2B property management software integrations (AppFolio, RentManager). However, this leaves 100% of independent renters without a landlord-initiated platform unserved.
