## 1. Summary — the shape of the field

The home repair estimation space is divided between B2B enterprise software ($99/mo contractor tools like Jobber/Housecall Pro) and simple single-trade calculators ([Paint Calculator Pro](https://apps.apple.com/app/paint-calculator-pro/id1453265893), [Tileo](https://apps.apple.com/app/tileo-tile-calculator/id1548234857)). Consumer-facing multi-trade estimators like [Renovise](https://apps.apple.com/app/renovise-remodel-tracker/id6475701878) and [Re:Build](https://apps.apple.com/app/rebuild-renovation-planner/id6444855219) focus on budget logging and project management rather than line-item contractor quote auditing.

**Price floor observed**: Free basic calculators on web/mobile; paid tier at $2.99–$4.99 one-time or $19.99/yr for Pro features across consumer tools.
