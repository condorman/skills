### 💡 ExposureGuide: Guided ERP Exercises with Live SUDS Graph & Fear Hierarchy

**Context Category**: Mobile App (iOS & Android) — Category F (Educational/Behavioral Protocols) / Category B (Micro-Productivity)
**Novelty Level**: **Prior Art Exists — Internal Duplicate + Confirmed External Commodity Mechanic** (not Unprecedented, not Unserved Niche Flank)

#### 1. Core Problem & Latent Friction
- **Discovered Friction**: People doing ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention) for OCD, phobias, and anxiety traditionally track SUDS (Subjective Units of Distress) ratings on paper during exposure exercises, and build a "fear hierarchy" of graded feared situations, usually under a therapist's guidance.
- **Target Audience**: People in active OCD/phobia/anxiety treatment, often working alongside a therapist, who want a lighter-weight self-guided tool between sessions.

#### 2. The "Why Now?" Factor (Tech Enabler)
- **Recent Tech Breakthrough**: None specific — this is a UI/charting/local-storage problem (line chart + list ordering + local timer), not something newly unlocked by AI/APIs.
- **Why It Couldn't Be Built Earlier**: It could have been built years ago. Multiple apps already do it (see below), some dating back well before this session's "why now."

#### 3. Novelty & Prior-Art Verification

**Prior-Art Search Results**: A 5-pass search found this mechanic is **already commoditized** by several live, published apps — not adjacent competitors, but direct mechanic-for-mechanic matches:

- **[Exposure - Face Your Fears](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/exposure-face-your-fears/id1135230491)** (iOS, free, no IAPs found): Lets users build a ranked fear hierarchy, then walk through exposures while **continuously tracking anxiety level in real time**, ending the exercise when distress drops to 20/100 or lower — i.e., the *exact* SUDS-habituation-during-exposure loop requested. Includes a repeated in-app warning: "We highly recommend using this app under the guidance of a mental health professional."
- **[FearTools - Anxiety Aid](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/feartools-anxiety-aid/id1179843607)** (iOS, free, no ads): Evidence-based app with a dedicated "Exposure" tool for gradual exposure therapy plus a GAD-7 severity tracker, thought diary, and breathing tool.
- **[OCD ERP: Exposure Therapy](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ocdserenity.exposurehierarchy)** (Android): Explicitly markets a "custom exposure hierarchy builder to design step-by-step plans for specific OCD fears."
- **[OCD Therapy Toolkit](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.olivier7355.ocdserenitymobile)** (Android): ERP toolkit to "track and manage your exposure hierarchy with customizable fear levels," 100+ pre-built hierarchies, Y-BOCS severity tracking.
- **[NOCD](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nocd-ocd-therapy-and-tools/id1063365447)**: The heavy, therapist-matched incumbent ($149–349/month out-of-pocket per [ChoosingTherapy's 2026 review](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/nocd-review/); ~$14 insured copay), offering live ERP sessions with licensed clinicians plus on-demand exercises.
- **[unstuck: OCD Therapy Tools](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/unstuck-ocd-therapy-tools/id6446218080)** and **[OCD Mantra](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ocd-mantra-ocd-treatment-app/id1610120131)**: Additional direct competitors, both carrying explicit "not a replacement for a therapist / does not replace the opinion of a medical professional" disclaimers.

**Patent Audit**: `patents.google.com` search for exposure-therapy + SUDS + mobile turned up **US11917250** and **US12322295** ("Audiovisual content selection"), which discuss using SUDS scores to drive adaptive exposure-content selection — an adjacent but more specific mechanism (content adapts to live SUDS) than the plain "hierarchy + live graph" requested here. Not a direct blocker for this concept as scoped, but worth flagging if the build later adds SUDS-driven adaptive content.

- **Originality Verdict**: **Flanking Existing Competitors — mechanic is commodity, not novel.** At least five live, published apps (three of them free) already ship "fear hierarchy + live/continuous SUDS anxiety tracking during exposure." This is not a flank of a heavy incumbent (the "$100+/mo NOCD" framing used in the original log entry #1) — the free direct competitors are the real comparison set, and the mechanic is undifferentiated from them.

#### 6. Legal & Regulatory Safety

- **Risk Tier**: 🔴 **Elevated** — per `legal_risk_playbook.md`, this idea squarely touches "therapy/CBT/exposure exercises" for OCD, phobias, and anxiety, an explicitly listed elevated-scrutiny domain.
- **Legal Risk Level**: **Moderate** (not "Very Low" — see justification below, grounded in cited research).
- **Notes** (elevated-tier, cited research):
  1. **Regulatory surface**: FDA's Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) framework applies when an app is "intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent a disease or condition." Per [npj Mental Health Research's review of FDA-authorized SaMD](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44184-025-00174-2) and related coverage, this **explicitly includes apps that "deliver cognitive behavioral therapy for anxiety or provide exposure therapy for PTSD."** Separately, of the roughly 20,000 mental-health/wellness apps on the App Store and Play Store, only about 5 hold FDA clearance/approval — meaning the overwhelming majority of comparable apps (including all the direct competitors found above) operate by staying *outside* the regulated-device boundary rather than by obtaining clearance.
  2. **How real competitors stay compliant**: None of the competitors found market themselves as treating or diagnosing OCD/phobias outright. They rely on (a) explicit non-therapy disclaimers and (b) framing as a self-help/practice companion, not a treatment: "unstuck" states "it is NOT technically delivering therapy itself and is NOT meant to replace a therapist"; "OCD Mantra" states "Any content or advice from this app does not replace the opinion of a medical professional"; "Exposure - Face Your Fears" repeats "We highly recommend using this app under the guidance of a mental health professional." FDA's own [January 2026 update to the "General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices" guidance](https://www.troutman.com/insights/fdas-2026-guidance-on-general-wellness-devices-policy-for-low-risk-devices/) reinforces that products intended "solely to promote a healthy lifestyle... or support general wellness without making disease-specific claims" can stay outside device regulation — the disclaimer-and-framing pattern above is exactly how these apps use that carve-out.
  3. **Concrete mitigation the candidate idea would need**: (a) frame the app explicitly as a self-practice companion for use alongside an existing therapist, never as a standalone "OCD/phobia treatment"; (b) avoid diagnostic language, symptom-severity scoring presented as clinical assessment (e.g. no unlicensed "Y-BOCS result" presented as a diagnosis), and any claim of treating/curing/preventing a disorder; (c) carry an explicit, prominent, repeated in-app disclaimer matching the pattern used by unstuck/OCD Mantra/Exposure - Face Your Fears; (d) keep all SUDS/hierarchy data on-device or encrypted-at-rest with no clinical data sharing, since sustained self-reported distress/health data could otherwise raise state health-privacy exposure beyond ordinary app privacy policy scope; (e) App Store/Play Store health-content review is a practical gate independent of FDA status — apps in this category get flagged for review if language implies treatment claims.
  4. **Would this push toward PIVOT/DISCARD on legal grounds alone?** Not by itself — the disclaimer-and-general-wellness-framing pattern is well-established and used successfully by multiple live competitors, so a correctly-scoped version of this app is legally buildable. The verdict here is DISCARDED on **commercial/competitive grounds** (Section 4), not primarily legal grounds — but the legal tier confirms this is not a "Very Low risk, ship it casually" category either; any future attempt at this concept must carry the mitigations above from day one, not bolt them on later.
