### 💡 MarketLedger MTD: Automated Making Tax Digital Quarterly Filing Engine for Multi-Marketplace UK Sellers

**Context Category**: B2B Micro-SaaS / Compliance Reporting Tool (Shopify App + standalone web app)
**Novelty Level**: Novel Combination / Unserved Niche Flank (not unprecedented — the MTD-compliance-software category is well established; the specific combination of marketplace-native settlement ingestion + HMRC ITSA category auto-mapping is not found bundled in any single competitor examined)

#### 1. Core Problem & Latent Friction
- **Discovered Friction**: From 6 April 2026 (already in force as of this evaluation, July 2026), UK sole traders/landlords with gross income over £50,000 must keep **digital records** and submit **quarterly updates** to HMRC via Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD ITSA) compatible software. HMRC's own guidance states a plain spreadsheet is **not sufficient on its own** — it must be paired with recognised bridging software. Generic MTD-compatible tools (Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Coconut, Bokio, Countingup) are built around **one connected business bank account** and categorize *net bank deposits* — they do not parse marketplace-native settlement/payout reports (gross sale price, referral/listing/payment-processing fees, promoted-listing ad spend, refunds, COGS) that a seller on 2+ marketplaces needs broken into HMRC's specific quarterly income/expense category boxes. Sellers are left manually reconciling Etsy/eBay/Amazon/Shopify exports into spreadsheets before or after their bank feed — precisely the kind of "combining 3+ tools / manual bridging work" friction this skill's discovery protocol targets.
- **Target Audience**: UK-resident sole traders / micro-partnerships selling across 2+ marketplaces (Etsy + eBay + Amazon + Shopify + Vinted/Depop/TikTok Shop is a common combination for resellers, handmade/vintage sellers, and small dropshippers) with gross turnover above the current £50k MTD threshold, doing their own books or using a low-cost bookkeeper.

#### 2. The "Why Now?" Factor (Tech Enabler)
- **Recent Tech/Regulatory Breakthrough**: (a) A hard, dated, penalty-backed regulatory deadline — MTD ITSA mandatory from 6 April 2026 for £50k+ turnover, phasing down to £30k in April 2027 and £20k in April 2028 — is *currently* creating fresh, forced demand, not speculative demand. (b) A newly-announced **embeddable third-party MTD ITSA submission API** (untied's API, explicitly built "to embed Making Tax Digital for Income Tax capability in third party products") now lets a small team submit directly to HMRC without independently completing HMRC's full multi-month software-recognition/testing process from scratch. (c) Marketplace settlement/financial-report APIs (Etsy Open API v3, eBay Finances API, Amazon SP-API Financial Events, Shopify Payouts API) are mature and documented.
- **Why It Couldn't Be Built Earlier**: The regulatory trigger itself is new (phase 1 of MTD ITSA only became mandatory this fiscal year). The embeddable-compliance-API layer (untied and similar) is also recent — without it, a solopreneur would have faced the same multi-month direct HMRC software-vendor accreditation process that historically only accounting-software incumbents completed.

#### 3. Novelty & Prior-Art Verification
- **Prior-Art Search Results**: Extensive live web research (see 3.2) found a dense, mature ecosystem of MTD-compliance software, but **no single tool combining marketplace-native settlement ingestion with automatic HMRC ITSA category mapping and submission**:
  - **GoSimpleTax** markets a landing page directly at eBay/Etsy/Amazon/Gumtree sellers, but a direct fetch of that page confirmed it is a **manual-entry, guided-questionnaire self-assessment tool** ("input your income and expenditure in real time," "walks you through the tax year") with no confirmed marketplace API import or automatic fee/COGS categorization. MTD ITSA is referenced only as a separate generic product line.
  - **Coconut, Bokio, Countingup** are HMRC-recognised MTD software with **bank-feed** automatic categorization — but they categorize net bank deposits, not gross marketplace sales/fees/refunds/COGS at order level, so a multi-marketplace seller still loses the granularity HMRC's category boxes require.
  - **untied** is an HMRC-recognised MTD ITSA infrastructure/API provider that other products can embed — this is adjacent *infrastructure*, not a marketplace-specific end-user competitor, and its existence is what validates the "why now" build path.
  - Also checked and confirmed generic (non-marketplace-native): 123 Sheets, MyTaxDigital, VitalTax (£25/yr), CHM Bridging Software, AbraTax, Anna Money.
  - US sales-tax automation platforms (TaxJar, Avalara, Zamp, Numeral) solve a **different** problem (US state sales-tax remittance) and do not collide with UK income-tax MTD reporting.
  - No relevant matches on Product Hunt or GitHub for "Making Tax Digital" + marketplace-seller automation.
- **Originality Verdict**: **Novel Combination / Unserved Niche Flank.** Not unprecedented — MTD compliance software is a well-established, competitive category — but the specific marketplace-native-settlement + HMRC-category-mapping bundle was not found assembled end-to-end by any examined competitor.

- **Proof 4 (AI Reliability >95%)**: **PASS — weaker, assertion/design-reasoning based.** The core pipeline is mostly deterministic rules-based mapping (fixed marketplace settlement-report schemas → HMRC's fixed, published category list), which is inherently easier to validate than open-ended LLM generation, and JSON-schema-validated structured output is feasible. However, no real accuracy benchmark was found or run for this specific classification task (e.g., ambiguous line items, multi-currency settlement edge cases) — this pass rests on architectural reasoning about the task's determinism, not on retrieved evidence of a working implementation's real-world accuracy. Mitigation: never auto-submit; always require explicit user confirmation before filing (see Section 6).
- **Proof 5 (Micro-Moat Defensibility)**: **PASS — moderate evidence, partly an absence-based (negative) finding.** Across the ~10 MTD-recognised vendors sampled (GoSimpleTax, Coconut, Bokio, Countingup, 123 Sheets, MyTaxDigital, VitalTax, CHM, AbraTax, Anna Money), none was confirmed to combine marketplace-native settlement ingestion with HMRC category auto-mapping — a real, evidenced gap, and the underlying moat (deep, maintained integrations with Etsy/eBay/Amazon/Shopify/Vinted/Depop, each with idiosyncratic settlement formats) qualifies as a valid "Deep API Integration" moat per `domain_saas.md`. - **Proof 6 (Status Quo Resistance)**: **PASS — strong retrieved evidence.** HMRC's own published guidance states plainly that a spreadsheet alone is **not** sufficient under MTD ITSA — it must be paired with recognised bridging software (sourced directly from GoCardless/1Office/IPSE). **Protocol Score: 6/6 → APPROVED**, with an explicit caveat: **3 of the 6 proofs (2, 4, 5) lean on reasoned inference or absence-based evidence rather than fully verified data.** Recommended before heavy build investment: (a) an independent keyword-volume check on the marketplace-seller long-tail, (b) a small pilot classification-accuracy test against 50–100 real settlement-report rows, and (c) direct confirmation of untied's (or an equivalent embeddable partner's) actual commercial/API-access terms.

#### 5. Solopreneur + AI Feasibility Stack
- **Recommended Tech Stack**: Next.js/Vite + Tailwind frontend, Supabase (Postgres) backend, marketplace connectors (Etsy Open API v3, eBay Finances API, Amazon SP-API Financial Events, Shopify Payouts/GraphQL Admin API), an embeddable HMRC-recognised MTD ITSA submission partner API (e.g. untied-class infra) rather than pursuing independent full HMRC software-vendor accreditation in v1.
- **AI Automation Scope**: LLM used only to *pre-suggest* category mapping for ambiguous/uncategorized settlement line items (schema-validated structured output); the money-critical submission path stays deterministic and always requires explicit user confirmation before anything is sent to HMRC — never a fully autonomous filing.
- **Solo Execution Time**: ~3–4 weeks for MVP (longer than a typical 1–2 week micro-SaaS build), explicitly because of (a) the embeddable MTD-API partner integration/commercial-agreement step and (b) OAuth developer-approval lead time from the Etsy/eBay/Amazon developer programs — both external dependencies outside the founder's direct control, flagged here rather than defaulting to an optimistic boilerplate estimate.

#### 6. Legal & Regulatory Safety
**Legal Risk Level: MODERATE** (explicitly *not* the default "Very Low/Zero" boilerplate — this product submits directly into a live government tax-compliance pipeline).
- **HMRC software-recognition dependency**: Must either become HMRC-recognised software (formal listing + sandbox testing) or route submissions through an already-recognised embeddable partner (e.g. untied) — a real gatekeeping dependency and external commercial relationship, not a formality; the launch timeline and even baseline viability partly depend on a third party's access terms.
- **Marketplace developer-program approval**: Etsy, eBay, and Amazon each require developer-app review/approval before granting settlement-data API access at scale, adding lead time and platform-policy risk (an app rejection or policy change is an external dependency outside solo-founder control).
- **Financial-data handling**: Processes sensitive financial/PII data — requires UK GDPR/DPA 2018-compliant handling; if bank-feed matching is ever added, must use an FCA-authorised Open Banking TPP (TrueLayer/Plaid/Yapily-class provider) rather than unauthorised bank scraping.
- **Liability & accuracy disclaimers**: Because output feeds a legal quarterly HMRC submission, miscategorized data could expose users to under/over-reported income and potential HMRC penalties. Requires clear "software, not tax advice" positioning (staying outside regulated tax-advice/accountancy-practice territory), a mandatory user-confirm step before every submission, a robust audit trail, and realistically professional-indemnity insurance before scaling past a handful of users — a genuine cost/time addition to the "solopreneur MVP" framing, not paperwork to wave away.
- **Net Assessment**: Buildable and legally sound as a solopreneur product **provided** it launches on an existing recognised submission-API partner rather than seeking independent full HMRC accreditation in v1, and **provided** submissions always require explicit user confirmation rather than full autonomy.
